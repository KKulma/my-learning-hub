These notes explain why a foundation language model is not, by itself, a reliable knowledge
system for enterprise applications. The course compares retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
and fine-tuning, then introduces retrieval-augmented fine-tuning (RAFT) as a way to combine
domain grounding with consistent model behavior.
